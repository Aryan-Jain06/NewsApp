How I work

Three habits that survive contact with production

Prove it, do not claim it

Every invariant worth stating gets an automated assertion. "No seat is sold twice" is a test that runs in CI, not a sentence in a README.

Pick the store that fits the shape

Redis for a claim that should expire on its own; Postgres for the record of truth and the uniqueness constraint. Two stores, each doing what it is actually good at.

Measure before optimising

p99 latency, delivery success rate, queue depth. The Java-to-Go migration happened because the numbers said so, not because Go is fashionable.
