What I work on

Three problems I keep coming back to

Distributed systems

Queues, stream processors and services that keep their invariants when a node dies mid-flight. At-least-once delivery, idempotency and state machines you can reason about.

Trading infrastructure

Real-time market ticks, strategy evaluation and order execution at a SEBI-registered platform. Latency and correctness are the product, not a nice-to-have.

Reliability engineering

Load tests, chaos tests and metrics that turn "it should work" into a number. If a claim is not asserted by a test, I do not put it in a README.
