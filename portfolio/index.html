About

Background

I finished my B.Tech in Computer Science at NSUT Delhi in May 2026, and I'm now an engineer at PolicyBazaar, working across .NET and Go — internal dashboards for the sales team, service optimisation, and an AI chatbot with sign-language support. Most of my own projects run the whole way through: a Go service underneath and a Next.js or React front end over it.

Before that I was on the backend team at Lemonn, part of the CoinSwitch group, working on the services that place and manage trades. Most of that was Go: a trading engine that evaluates strategies against live market ticks and candle data, a trailing stop loss service on Kubernetes that uses Kafka to keep up with thousands of concurrent orders, and a replay framework that runs strategies against past market data so they can be tested without real money. I also moved one of the core services off Java and onto Go.

Earlier I spent a summer at Padhai Help building Spring Boot APIs and designing the schema behind course progression. Outside work I solve algorithm problems — a little over a thousand between LeetCode and Codeforces — and I play chess; I headed the team at NSUT.

HookRelay

A standalone webhook delivery service. Applications publish events to it; it signs each delivery with HMAC, retries failing endpoints with exponential backoff, and moves deliveries that run out of attempts to a dead-letter queue that can be inspected and replayed.

Load test: 10,000 events fanned out to 30,000 deliveries with none lost, including a run where the worker was killed mid-delivery.
